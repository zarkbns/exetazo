How it works

A security audit, in four steps.

No chatbot, no summary to trust on faith. A rule-based scanner that shows its work.

01

Open any legal page

Terms of service, privacy policies, subscription agreements — the fine print everywhere.

02

Hit Scan

The extension extracts the page's legal text, strips navigation and cookie noise, and analyzes it in seconds.

03

Read the report

A security-style score out of 100 with severity-ranked findings — critical clauses first.

04

See the clause

Click a finding and the exact clause highlights on the page, with why it matters and what to watch for.

Laws & rules

15 clause types it watches for.

Every category has an explicit definition and severity logic — no black boxes. Open one to see what it means.

The company can change the terms at any time — without asking you, and sometimes without telling you. Whatever you agreed to can become something else entirely.

You give up the right to sue in court. Disputes go to a private arbitrator chosen under rules the company picked.

You can't join a class lawsuit. If the company harms thousands of people a little each, no one can pool their claims.

Your subscription renews on its own, without a clear reminder — and the clock on any refund window keeps running.

Cancelling requires a phone call, a mailed letter, or a maze of steps — while signing up took one click.

The company limits what it owes you when things go wrong — capping damages or disclaiming warranties in sweeping terms.

You agree to cover the company's legal costs — even for disputes that aren't your fault.

Your data can be shared with a long list of third parties — affiliates, partners, "service providers" — under vague purposes.

The terms explicitly allow selling your personal information, or treat "sharing" and "selling" as the same thing.

It's unclear how long your data is kept — or deletion only applies to some of it, "where required by law".

The company can suspend or delete your account for vague reasons — "harmful conduct", "any reason" — with no appeal.

Disputes must be fought in a specific court or location — often one far from you and convenient for the company.

Unclear pricing, surprise charges, or the right to change prices mid-subscription with minimal notice.

The company claims broad rights over your content — a license so wide it can outlive your account, or read like ownership.

The document promises one thing in one section and takes it back in another. Exetazo cross-checks sections to catch the contradiction.

Why Exetazo

Audit-grade by design.

Borrowed from smart-contract security: deterministic rules, quoted evidence, reproducible results.

Evidence over claims

Every finding quotes the exact clause text and where it sits in the document. Nothing is flagged without a source you can check yourself.

Rules, not vibes

Severity comes from a rules engine, not AI confidence. Scan the same document twice and you get the same score — that's the point.

100 − 20·critical − 10·high − 5·medium − 2·low

Nothing kept

Page text is analyzed and discarded. No documents stored, no accounts, no tracking. Credentials only ever live server-side.
